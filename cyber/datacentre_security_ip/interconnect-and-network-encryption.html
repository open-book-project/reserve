    Chapter 10: Interconnect and Network Encryption¶

    An AI data centre has at least six distinct encrypted-link technologies, each with its own key management. Data is protected on the wire between the client and the edge, across the fabric, between data centres, between a CPU and its DRAM, between GPUs, and between a device and its host. This chapter maps the technologies onto the data path and lists the standards and the silicon and IP vendors behind each.

    10.1 The link-encryption map¶

    Figure 10.1 follows the data path from a client to a remote GPU. From the client to the edge load balancer or API gateway the link uses TLS 1.3 or QUIC with hybrid PQC key exchange. Within the data-centre fabric (leaf and spine) it uses mutual TLS, IPsec or PSP, and MACsec on individual links. Between data centres it uses OTN or 400ZR encryption. Inside the server, a DPU or SuperNIC applies MACsec, IPsec or PSP inline, the CPU encrypts DRAM with AES-XTS (TME-MK, SME or Arm MEC), PCIe IDE with TDISP or bounce buffers protects the CPU-GPU link, NVLink encryption protects GPU-to-GPU traffic, CXL IDE protects CXL memory, and PCIe IDE, NVMe/TCP TLS and self-encrypting drives protect storage. Scale-out RDMA between GPUs uses IPsec or PSP on the SuperNIC, or the Ultra Ethernet transport security sublayer. Each hop has its own key-management plane: TLS PKI, IKE or PSP master keys, MKA (802.1X), IDE_KM over SPDM, and NVLink key exchange under the GPU RoT.

    10.3 Summary¶

    • An AI data centre has at least six distinct encrypted-link technologies, each with its own key management.
    • PCIe and CXL IDE (AES-GCM, with TDISP), NVLink and UALinkSec, MACsec, IPsec and PSP, the Ultra Ethernet security sublayer, and layer-1 optical encryption cover the device, fabric and inter-site links.
    • Memory encryption (TME-MK, SME or SEV, MEC) protects DRAM, and self-encrypting drives and OCP L.O.C.K. protect storage at rest.
    • The IDE engines are licensable IP from Synopsys, Rambus, Cadence and others, which is why they appear in so many chips.

    10.4 Exercises¶

    1. Name the technology that protects (a) CPU-to-GPU traffic over PCIe, (b) GPU-to-GPU traffic inside a rack, (c) traffic between two data centres.

      Answer

      (a) PCIe IDE with TDISP, or bounce buffers on Hopper. (b) NVLink encryption, or UALinkSec in the open standard. (c) Layer-1 OTN or 400ZR and 800ZR encryption, or MACsec over DWDM.

    2. Why does each hop in the map need its own key-management plane?

      Answer

      The hops use different protocols with different trust endpoints: TLS uses a PKI, IPsec and PSP use IKE or master keys, MACsec uses MKA, IDE keys are programmed with IDE_KM over SPDM, and NVLink exchanges keys under the GPU RoT.

    3. Which cipher family do almost all of the link encryptions in the table use, and which one is the exception for storage and memory?

      Answer

      AES-GCM for the links. Memory and storage use AES-XTS.
