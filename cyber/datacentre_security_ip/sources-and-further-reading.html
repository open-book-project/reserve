    Sources and Further Reading¶

    Sources¶

    The survey was compiled from public specifications and vendor documentation, checked in October 2026. The principal sources are listed here.

    • NIST SP 800-193, Platform Firmware Resiliency Guidelines.
    • NIST FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (August 2024); NIST IR 8547 (initial public draft).
    • NSA, Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite 2.0 advisory (2022, updated).
    • DMTF SPDM (DSP0274) and secured messages (DSP0277).
    • TCG DICE Architectures work group.
    • OCP Security project, DC-SCM and S.A.F.E.
    • CHIPS Alliance, announcement of the Caliptra 2.1 RTL release, and the Caliptra repository.
    • OCP L.O.C.K. (OSFC 2025 talk, J. Andersen and E. Eilertson).
    • AMD, "Integrating Project Caliptra into AMD's product lineup"; "OpenPRoT: Building a secure and transparent foundation for platform root of trust"; the AMD SEV-TIO white paper.
    • Google Open Source Blog, "OpenTitan shipping in production" (March 2026); lowRISC and the OpenTitan project.
    • Microsoft Azure, "Protecting Azure infrastructure from silicon to systems".
    • AWS, The Security Design of the AWS Nitro System (whitepaper).
    • Apple Security Research, Private Cloud Compute.
    • Trade-press coverage of NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72, AMD Helios MI400 (Hot Chips 2026) and Intel TDX Connect.
    • Vendor announcements and briefs: Microchip TS1800, Lattice MachXO5-NX TDQ, ASPEED AST2700, Axiado AX3080, Synopsys CXL 3.x IDE security module, Rambus root-of-trust IP, CAST KiviPQC.
    • ABI Research, on open-source frameworks displacing proprietary security IP blocks in the cloud.
    • TEE.fail (Georgia Tech, Purdue and Synkhronix, 2025).
    • RAND, Securing AI Model Weights (RR-A2849-1, 2024).

    Verify before you rely

    This book is an independent technical survey based on public information. Product capabilities, certifications and roadmaps change frequently, and some 2026 items are announced rather than generally available. Verify specifics with vendors and primary specifications before you rely on them for design, procurement or compliance decisions.
